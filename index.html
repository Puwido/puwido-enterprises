Approach

Every request is checked on the server. Every setting lives in one config file. Every name says exactly what it does.

That is the standard the work is held to: code the next developer can pick up without guessing, and systems an exploiter can’t talk their way around.

Most of it runs on the server, out of sight. The result shows up as games that stay fair, progress that doesn’t vanish, and staff who can act without editing a line of code.
